§ 01 — The Studio
A letter from us.
On why we exist
Dastardly & Muttley Elysium Gallery Studios Swansea, Wales

Independent businesses carry stories that corporate brands pay millions to fake. The corner café that's been there for twenty years. The band that's just about to break through. The market trader who genuinely knows everything about what they sell.

All they need is the right visual language to tell that story. That's what we do — web design, graphic design, photography, print. The complete picture, built around what makes you genuinely different.

No templates. No off-the-shelf solutions. No agency theatre. We take on a small number of projects at a time so every one gets the full version of us.

§ 02 — How we work
Things we believe.
Studio principles
01

Local is not small.

Independent doesn't mean underpowered. The best work we've made has been for businesses two miles from here — and it's gone up against national brands without blinking. Scale is not the point. Distinctiveness is.

02

Opinion over options.

We don't show you forty-seven directions and ask which one you like. We show you the right one and tell you why it works. You can push back; we'll fight our corner. That tension is where the good stuff comes from.

03

One brief, one system.

Web, print, photography — all under one roof, all from one brief. A brand that holds together from the screen to the shopfront to the side of a van, without you having to herd three separate agencies.

04

Nothing off-the-shelf.

Every project is built from scratch. We don't have templates, frameworks, or preset solutions. If someone else could have made the same thing, it's not done.
