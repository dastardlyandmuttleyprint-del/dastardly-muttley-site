§ The Brief

Sandro Ford is a Welsh comedian with a very specific grievance: he orders a pilsner and they bring it in the wrong glass. Wrong Glass is his show. Working with Inspiration Swansea, we made him the merch — and the merch had to be the joke.

§ 01 — The Object

The object is the bit.

Not a pilsner glass. Not a pint glass. The wrong glass — that's the whole point. Sandro's portrait stares up through the beer. The type is big, bold, slightly chaotic.

Fig. 01 — Custom printed glasswarePhotography · Dastardly & Muttley
§ 02 — Approach

Close enough to feel off.

The glass performs the show.

The brief writes itself once you understand the joke. Something that sits in the uncanny valley of pub glassware — close enough to feel off.

The glass doesn't explain the show. It performs it.
