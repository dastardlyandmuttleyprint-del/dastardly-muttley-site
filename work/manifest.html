§ The Brief

Manifest is a Welsh açaí bowl brand with a genuine health story to tell. We worked across every brand touchpoint — the website, the wall, the screens above the counter, and the tees on the staff's backs. The same voice, everywhere.

§ 01 — Website Design

Life's too short for boring bowls.

The website sells the proposition as much as the product. The hero makes the case immediately — bold, playful, confident. Scrolling deeper it explains the science: gut health, antioxidants, brain function. Built to make a health-forward brand feel desirable, not dutiful.

Built from the brand up.

Every typographic decision on the site mirrors the print work — Space Grotesk for the confident, wide-set headlines; Instrument Serif italic for warmth. No web-safe defaults, no template shortcuts.

The menu section uses a card grid that rotates product categories without cluttering the screen. The science story scrolls as a slow editorial sequence — one claim at a time, backed by illustration, never a bullet list.

§ 02 — In-Store Posters

The science, not the lecture.

Exploded product photography with floating benefit callouts — antioxidants, gut health, mood support. The food does the talking. Posters that make people want the bowl, not reach for a textbook.

Fig. 02 — In-store poster set, A2 Photography & art direction · Dastardly & Muttley
§ 02b — On the Street

The brand, in the street.

Beyond the four walls.

The poster system extended outside — A-boards on the pavement, window graphics, branded bunting. The same visual language taking the brand to the high street.

Consistent from the inside out.

§ 03 — Digital Menus

The brand, above the counter.

The screens above the till weren't an afterthought. Same typography, same visual language as the website and the posters. Whether scrolling from home or standing at the counter, the brand holds.

Designed as carefully as everything else.

Most cafés bolt a stock menu template onto a TV and call it done. We treated the screens as a primary brand surface — set in the same Space Grotesk and Instrument Serif as the rest of the system, with rotating product cards and a slow editorial rhythm.

§ 04 — Staffwear

Raise the standard.

Staff tees are usually an afterthought. We designed them as a brand touchpoint — quality blanks, the "Açaí Addict Club" graphic on the back. The brand's playful side, worn by the people who believe in it most.
