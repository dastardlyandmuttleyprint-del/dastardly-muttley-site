§ 03 — Process
How it goes.
A four-stage system
01/

Brief & Discover

A real conversation, not a form. We dig into the business, the market, and what makes you genuinely different from everyone else doing the same thing on your high street.

~ 1 — 2 weeks
02/

Strategy & Direction

Before a pixel moves, we lock the direction. Visual territory, tone, references — the thinking that makes everything else actually land.

~ 1 week
03/

Design & Build

The full system — mark, colour, type, web, print. Every element built to work together. We iterate until it's exactly right and you can't imagine it any other way.

~ 4 — 8 weeks
04/

Deliver & Launch

A handoff that actually makes sense — files, guidelines, and a system you can run yourself. We stay on hand for whatever comes next, because most projects don't end at launch.
