§ 04 — Process How it goes.
A four-stage system
01/

Brief & Discover

A real conversation, not a form. We dig into the business, the market, and what makes you genuinely different.

~ 1 — 2 weeks
02/

Strategy & Direction

Before a pixel moves, we lock the direction — visual territory, tone, references.

~ 1 week
03/

Design & Build

The full system — logo, colour, type, web, print. We iterate until it's exactly right.

~ 4 — 8 weeks
04/

Deliver & Launch

Files, brand guidelines, and a system you can run yourself. We stay on hand for what comes next.
